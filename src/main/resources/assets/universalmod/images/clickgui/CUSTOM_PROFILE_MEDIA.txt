Custom ClickGUI profile media
=============================
At runtime place files here:
.minecraft/universalmod/clickgui/avatar.png
.minecraft/universalmod/clickgui/cover.png

The files hot-reload while the ClickGUI is open.
You can also drag an image onto the profile card:
- drop over the avatar area: installs avatar.png
- drop over the rest of the profile card: installs cover.png
